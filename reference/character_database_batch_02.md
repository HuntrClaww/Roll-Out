# Character Database — Batch 02

Batch 02 expands the active roster from 20 to 40 characters. These are proposed characters, not locked canon. The batch focuses on regional workers, faction knights, challenge contacts, rivals, living environments, and creator-era systems so the world grows horizontally rather than adding only more bosses.

## New regional and service characters

- **Ora Bridgekeeper** — Sky Dominion bridge inspector who can warn about altered vertical routes.
- **Ashen Mira** — Ash Banner heat-monitoring specialist who provides practical volcanic preparation.
- **Vesper Melt** — Frost Reign thaw-settlement guide preserving seasonal route memories.
- **Quiet Ell** — Null Veil observer who records sounds arriving from the wrong direction.
- **Stamp Echo** — Convergence League duplicate checkpoint clerk who turns scheduling errors into useful notices.
- **Needle Nell** — Amber Circuit precision-repair specialist who complements Pip’s improvisational crafting.
- **Ember Lark** — Ash Banner rest-platform host who turns rumours into songs.
- **Rime Choir** — Collective Frost Reign route-maintenance identity using synchronized markers.
- **Tally Nine** — Null Veil counter who records routes appearing more often than they should.
- **Ribbon Jan** — Convergence League decoration worker who foreshadows events through banners.
- **Hollow Gear** — Outdated maintenance machine continuing to repair unfinished creator-era systems.
- **Copper Echo** — Signal tower whose personality formed after repeated finish-line surges.
- **Fable Fern** — Living shortcut garden that protects beginners and indicates safer routes.

## New rivals and faction members

- **Hedge Lumen** — Friendly Open Circuit rival focused on alternate lines.
- **Iron Pollen** — Amber Circuit material-science rival who studies magnetic stone formations.
- **Morrow Dash** — Crimson Zero courier who races near changing weather without making strict timing mandatory.

## New faction knights

- **Kite Warden** — Sky Dominion escort knight who illustrates the difference between a skilled knight and a specialized boss.
- **Ash Cadet** — Eager Ash Banner trainee learning Cinder Axis’s preparation methods.
- **Snow Cadet** — Careful Frost Reign trainee learning Glacier Sigil’s route marks.
- **Veil Runner** — Null Veil scout who follows unstable routes and reports incomplete results.

## Content-balance rule

Batch 02 adds no new boss. Its purpose is to make existing regions, factions, services, and mysteries feel inhabited before the next boss expansion. The matching data is in `src/story/characterDatabaseBatch02.ts`, and the combined roster is exposed through `CHARACTER_DATABASE_ALL`.
