# Character Database — Batch 03

Batch 03 expands the roster from 40 to 60 characters. It focuses on old creator systems, route evidence, environmental records, unusual objects that may be alive, and secondary perspectives on the dimensional mystery.

## Narrative groups

### Archive and mystery figures

- The Marble Archivist
- Sunken Scribe
- Choir of Static
- Mirror Mara
- Tally Nine
- Ghost Lap
- Vaultkeeper Io
- Copper Echo

These characters carry heavier lore weight, but none of them is allowed to explain the whole mystery alone. Each preserves, measures, hears, repeats, or protects one incomplete piece of evidence.

### Practical regional characters

- Undertow Porter
- Lantern May
- Rivet King
- Faraday Finch
- Salt Analog
- Rail Bell
- Morrow Mint
- Pocket Storm

These characters keep the world grounded in transport, repairs, food, weather, geology, and ordinary social life.

### Challenge and faction figures

- Nettle Judge
- Quartz Quick
- Ashen Foil
- Cogline
- Ice Thread

These characters add fairness review, rival variety, and knight perspectives without adding another boss.

## Integration status

Batch 03 now has visual profiles, regional and stage encounter conditions, selected service roles, and initial dialogue scenes for its most important characters. Mystery-heavy characters remain deliberately incomplete in their explanations so they add clues without resolving the entire creator mystery.

The batch is ready for a final playtest-oriented balance review. Batch 04 should not be authored until that review confirms that the 60-character roster is discoverable without overwhelming the player.
