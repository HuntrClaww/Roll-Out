# Character Database — Batch 01

Status: proposed character layer for the first twenty named or role-bearing characters.

This batch expands the world without locking every detail as permanent canon. The characters use the requested structural idea of small, memorable helpers and acquaintances alongside more important racers, guides, rivals, and bosses. They are not copies of characters from *Shadow Fight*; that comparison is used only as a reference for narrative scale and frequency of appearance.

## Design rules for this batch

- The protagonist, Static, the major bosses, and the recurring rivals carry the heavier story weight.
- Most of the remaining characters make the world feel inhabited, deliver events, support upgrades, explain practical systems, or offer clues without becoming central narrators.
- Bosses are representatives of places, cultures, or systems. They are not automatically villains.
- Clues are deliberately distributed across ordinary interactions. A helpful character may know one useful fact without understanding the whole mystery.
- Characters can recur at flexible intervals. No interaction should require a strict real-time schedule to remain accessible.

## Character roster

### 1. The Main Character

- **Role:** central protagonist
- **Origin:** An ordinary racing world whose larger history is not yet known.
- **Personality:** Cheerful, laid-back, curious, funny, and quietly persistent.
- **Traits:** Asks simple questions with large consequences; keeps moving under pressure; competes without being cruel.
- **Strengths:** Adaptability, recovery, and social openness.
- **Weaknesses:** Impulsive curiosity, underestimating danger, and not always noticing personal significance.
- **Story use:** The player's viewpoint and the person through whom the tangled chain becomes visible.
- **First contact:** Already present at the opening race.

### 2. Static

- **Role:** major guide
- **Origin:** Unknown; apparently assembled, assigned, or awakened for observation and route support.
- **Personality:** Analytical, observant, literal, patient until ignored, and dryly funny.
- **Traits:** Records anomalies, notices performance inconsistencies, and asks precise follow-up questions.
- **Strengths:** Measurement, pattern recognition, and risk assessment.
- **Weaknesses:** Takes incomplete data seriously, struggles with jokes, and cannot explain what has not been measured.
- **Story use:** The first character to notice that the protagonist's small extra efforts do not fit expected calculations.
- **First contact:** A practical guide during the opening race.

### 3. Aether Sovereign

- **Role:** major boss of the Sky Dominion
- **Origin:** A high-altitude racer accustomed to unstable routes and changing winds.
- **Personality:** Composed, friendly when challenged, and ceremonial without being cruel.
- **Traits:** Offers answers after races, treats weather as conversation, and prefers elegant routes.
- **Strengths:** Control, prediction, and environmental adaptation.
- **Weaknesses:** Overcomplicates simple choices and assumes others enjoy precision as much as he does.
- **Story use:** Confirms that finish lines can behave unusually while refusing to explain everything.
- **First contact:** A race offered as the price of a useful direction.

### 4. Cinder Axis

- **Role:** major boss of the Ash Banner
- **Origin:** Shaped by hot rock, unstable channels, and a culture that respects preparation.
- **Personality:** Intense, practical, protective, and easily impressed by competence.
- **Traits:** Tests heat preparation, notices equipment details, and speaks plainly.
- **Strengths:** Heat tolerance, durability, and route discipline.
- **Weaknesses:** Poor patience for improvisation and a tendency to take preventable mistakes personally.
- **Story use:** Shows that a boss can be a demanding teacher and faction representative without being a villain.
- **First contact:** A volcanic challenge with a clear preparation requirement.

### 5. Glacier Sigil

- **Role:** major boss of the Frost Reign
- **Origin:** An old ice-route specialist who remembers paths that no longer exist.
- **Personality:** Quiet, precise, kind, and difficult to read.
- **Traits:** Speaks in route metaphors, keeps old maps, and waits before answering.
- **Strengths:** Surface control, patience, and memory.
- **Weaknesses:** Slow to trust new explanations and capable of mistaking caution for certainty.
- **Story use:** Connects environmental history to the question of whether tracks remember previous racers.
- **First contact:** A frozen route requiring control rather than speed alone.

### 6. Rift Echelon

- **Role:** major boss of the Null Veil
- **Origin:** A racer associated with routes that are difficult to classify or repeat.
- **Personality:** Clever, sly, and playfully evasive.
- **Traits:** Answers questions with tests, remembers embarrassing details, and enjoys uncertainty.
- **Strengths:** Risk reading, misdirection, and fast adaptation.
- **Weaknesses:** Creates confusion for entertainment and sometimes believes their own tricks.
- **Story use:** Introduces contradictory explanations without forcing the story to choose one immediately.
- **First contact:** A challenge where the obvious route is not necessarily the useful route.

### 7. The Circuit Steward

- **Role:** major boss and Convergence League coordinator
- **Origin:** Responsible for coordinating racers who arrive from incompatible routes.
- **Personality:** Organized, welcoming, overworked, and proud of bad signage.
- **Traits:** Values team coordination, knows many names, and keeps incomplete schedules.
- **Strengths:** Coordination, networking, and logistics.
- **Weaknesses:** Loses track of causality and treats obvious system failures as temporary.
- **Story use:** Bridges team competition, dimensional traffic, and old unfinished systems.
- **First contact:** A team challenge requiring cooperation rather than one perfect racer.

### 8. Basil Varn

- **Role:** recurring rival of the Amber Circuit
- **Origin:** A route-focused racer who believes consistency beats spectacle.
- **Personality:** Competitive, tactical, and polite until losing.
- **Traits:** Studies opponents, keeps score honestly, and returns after setbacks.
- **Strengths:** Planning, surface reading, and reliability.
- **Weaknesses:** Overthinks improvisation and hates losing to accidents.
- **Story use:** A rival who can become an ally or influential racer while remaining independent of the main plot.
- **First contact:** A fair race followed by an argument about whether the finish line moved.

### 9. Cinder Vale

- **Role:** recurring rival of Crimson Zero
- **Origin:** A fast racer who treats risk as a form of self-expression.
- **Personality:** Bold, impatient, charismatic, and secretly thoughtful.
- **Traits:** Takes shortcuts, learns after failure, and collects stories from dangerous tracks.
- **Strengths:** Speed, bravery, and momentum management.
- **Weaknesses:** Poor restraint and a habit of confusing confidence with preparation.
- **Story use:** Demonstrates how a rival can develop independently and carry information without becoming a lore narrator.
- **First contact:** An aggressive challenge where the fastest route is not the safest route.

### 10. Sly-Mark

- **Role:** recurring acquaintance and event contact
- **Origin:** A collection of temporary tracks and unofficial shortcuts.
- **Personality:** Smooth, helpful when profitable, and dramatic about ordinary information.
- **Traits:** Knows who is arriving, sells directions, and never admits to guessing.
- **Strengths:** Social awareness, timing, and route-finding.
- **Weaknesses:** Reliability depends on mood, and simple facts become embellished.
- **Story use:** A small recurring contact who can notify the player of events without carrying the main plot.
- **First contact:** Appears beside a sign that clearly does not belong to him.

### 11. Mosswheel

- **Role:** supporting world-worker
- **Origin:** A living track region where vegetation responds to vibration.
- **Personality:** Patient, quietly humorous, and protective of small creatures.
- **Traits:** Repairs living surfaces, recognizes footsteps, and dislikes unnecessary noise.
- **Strengths:** Environmental knowledge, care, and observation.
- **Weaknesses:** Slow to react to urgent changes and assumes everyone notices subtle movement.
- **Story use:** Introduces biology through practical track maintenance rather than exposition.
- **First contact:** Asks the player to stop rolling over a sleeping patch of terrain.

### 12. Pip Salvage

- **Role:** recurring friend and upgrade helper
- **Origin:** A scrapyard between commonly used finish-line routes.
- **Personality:** Enthusiastic, inventive, and easily distracted.
- **Traits:** Repurposes broken equipment, names every tool, and finds useful objects by accident.
- **Strengths:** Crafting, improvisation, and resourcefulness.
- **Weaknesses:** Keeps unsafe prototypes and forgets where important parts came from.
- **Story use:** Supports upgrades and may accidentally uncover creator-era components.
- **First contact:** Offers a grip modification after mistaking the protagonist for a delivery.

### 13. Brassettle

- **Role:** supporting world-worker
- **Origin:** Old maintenance corridors beneath the first connected tracks.
- **Personality:** Fussy, proud, and easily offended by incorrect labels.
- **Traits:** Paints signs, maintains route markers, and knows which repairs were never finished.
- **Strengths:** Memory, organization, and technical craft.
- **Weaknesses:** Cannot explain symbols simply and refuses to remove obsolete signs.
- **Story use:** Connects comedy and ordinary maintenance work to the larger creator mystery.
- **First contact:** Insists that a sign saying “temporary” has been temporary for a very long time.

### 14. Nix Weather

- **Role:** recurring event contact
- **Origin:** A high pass where wind patterns change faster than official forecasts.
- **Personality:** Alert, chatty, and dramatic about measurements.
- **Traits:** Announces environmental changes, collects failed predictions, and likes visible data.
- **Strengths:** Forecasting, communication, and rapid observation.
- **Weaknesses:** Overstates danger and updates forecasts too often.
- **Story use:** Notifies the player of environmental events while keeping scientific information approachable.
- **First contact:** Warns the player about wind, then admits the wind was not predicted.

### 15. Loop

- **Role:** recurring event contact and checkpoint clerk
- **Origin:** A checkpoint station that appears in more places than its map allows.
- **Personality:** Professional, calm, and quietly confused.
- **Traits:** Checks entries, remembers previous attempts, and never comments on the station moving.
- **Strengths:** Routine, record keeping, and fairness.
- **Weaknesses:** Trusts forms more than observations and cannot decide which station is the original.
- **Story use:** Delivers challenge requirements and hints that locations may not be as fixed as they appear.
- **First contact:** Requests a checkpoint stamp from a race that has not started yet.

### 16. Mallow

- **Role:** background acquaintance
- **Origin:** A rest stop between volcanic and ordinary terrain.
- **Personality:** Warm, observant, and unimpressed by famous racers.
- **Traits:** Serves heat-safe snacks, remembers preferences, and hears rumors without chasing them.
- **Strengths:** Hospitality, social memory, and emotional steadiness.
- **Weaknesses:** Avoids direct confrontation and occasionally repeats a rumor incorrectly.
- **Story use:** Makes the world feel inhabited and delivers small clues through ordinary conversation.
- **First contact:** Offers the protagonist something cool and asks whether the track was supposed to do that.

### 17. Quarry

- **Role:** supporting world-worker and repair specialist
- **Origin:** A rocky route repeatedly damaged by ambitious racers.
- **Personality:** Direct, practical, and secretly sentimental about tracks.
- **Traits:** Repairs collision zones, tests materials by sound, and knows which obstacles are intentional.
- **Strengths:** Construction, material knowledge, and patience.
- **Weaknesses:** Does not understand dimensional markings and refuses to call a repair finished until it is overbuilt.
- **Story use:** Connects material science and track history without turning either into a lecture.
- **First contact:** Asks the player to report whether a boulder moved or the track moved around it.

### 18. Vela

- **Role:** recurring friend and cartographer
- **Origin:** A map room assembled from route fragments gathered after dimensional transitions.
- **Personality:** Focused, curious, and patient with questions but not with bad maps.
- **Traits:** Compares route patterns, marks contradictions, and keeps maps with missing centers.
- **Strengths:** Cartography, analysis, and long-term memory.
- **Weaknesses:** Follows patterns too eagerly and struggles to accept that a map may be wrong.
- **Story use:** Turns the finish-line mystery into a gradual pattern of locations rather than an immediate revelation.
- **First contact:** Recognizes a symbol from the protagonist’s route but cannot place where she first saw it.

### 19. Drift

- **Role:** recurring acquaintance and beginner racer
- **Origin:** A beginner circuit that shares training routes with the protagonist’s early races.
- **Personality:** Eager, honest, easily impressed, and occasionally competitive.
- **Traits:** Copies techniques, asks direct questions, and returns with small improvements.
- **Strengths:** Learning, enthusiasm, and persistence.
- **Weaknesses:** Takes advice too literally and attempts advanced maneuvers too early.
- **Story use:** Shows how the protagonist’s reputation begins affecting ordinary racers.
- **First contact:** Asks how the protagonist stayed moving after a failed turn.

### 20. Rook

- **Role:** supporting event contact and rumor collector
- **Origin:** A crowded exchange route where rumors move faster than racers.
- **Personality:** Confident, sociable, and wrong often enough to be useful.
- **Traits:** Collects stories, connects unrelated details, and sometimes reports a true thing for the wrong reason.
- **Strengths:** Networking, recall, and creative interpretation.
- **Weaknesses:** Cannot separate rumor from evidence and gets bored by verification.
- **Story use:** Provides contradictory clues and lets the player decide what deserves investigation.
- **First contact:** Claims to know why finish lines move, then admits he was talking about a different finish line.

## Implementation boundary

The next character sub-phase should connect these records to faction events, relationship changes, encounter availability, and dialogue variants before expanding the roster toward the planned 100–110 character range.
